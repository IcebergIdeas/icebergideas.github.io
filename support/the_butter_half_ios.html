Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scale a recipe?

Go to the Scale tab, choose a recipe, and change the batch size. Ingredient amounts will update automatically.

How do I create or edit recipes?

Go to the Recipes tab to create a new recipe, add/remove ingredients, and save your updates.

Why can’t I choose certain batch sizes?

For quantity-based ingredients (like eggs), The Butter Half uses practical increments so amounts stay usable for home baking.

Can I switch units?

Yes. Open Settings and choose between Imperial or Metric units.
