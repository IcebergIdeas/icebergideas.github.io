Privacy Policy for The Butter Half (iOS)

Effective date: May 21, 2026

Iceberg Ideas Inc (“we,” “our,” or “us”) built The Butter Half as a recipe scaling app for bakers. This app is designed to work locally on your device, ensuring your data remains private and under your control.

1. Information We Collect

The Butter Half is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from within the app.

2. Recipe and Usage Data

Recipes, ingredient values, and settings you enter in the app are stored locally on your device for app functionality. We do not have access to this data.

3. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

  • The Butter Half does not use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The Butter Half does not use cross-app tracking.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties because we do not collect personal data in the app.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Butter Half does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

Because the app stores data locally on your device, your data security also depends on your device security settings (passcode, backups, etc.).

7. Third-Party Services

If Apple provides platform-level services (such as App Store, crash diagnostics, or device analytics), those services are governed by Apple’s policies and settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at the same URL with a revised effective date.
