RigBrief Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

App: RigBrief
Developer: Iceberg Ideas Inc.

This Privacy Policy explains how RigBrief handles information. RigBrief is designed as a local-first performance utility. We do not operate a RigBrief account system or cloud backend for your song library.

Information We Collect

RigBrief does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location for app functionality.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking in RigBrief.

Google Play Data safety summary: RigBrief does not collect or share user data with the developer. Data you enter in the app is stored locally on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device (Android)

RigBrief stores the following locally on your Android device:

  • Songs you add or import
  • Named setlists and setlist membership
  • Per-song transpose settings
  • Per-song performance notes
  • Home screen display preferences

This data is saved in on-device app storage (for example, app preferences and local files). It remains on your device unless you delete the app, remove content inside the app, or export it.

Network Access and Permissions

RigBrief does not request the INTERNET permission and does not connect to RigBrief servers to sync your library.

The app may use standard Android system features you initiate yourself, such as the document picker for import and the share sheet for export.

Import and Export

You may import or export library JSON files using Android’s file picker and share features. These actions are initiated by you. RigBrief does not automatically upload your library to our servers.

Device Backup

Depending on your device and Android version, app data may be included in Google’s device backup or transfer features. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by a RigBrief cloud service.

Bundled Content

The app includes three RigBrief demo songs. Any other charts, lyrics, or setlists in your library are supplied by you through import or manual entry.

User-Supplied Content

You are responsible for the content you import or create, including compliance with copyright and licensing for chord charts and song material you store in the app.

Children's Privacy

RigBrief is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because your library is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on your device lock screen, biometric settings, and protections provided by Android and your device manufacturer.

Your Choices

  • Delete songs or setlists inside the app
  • Export your library before making major changes
  • Remove all app data by deleting RigBrief from your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are posted.
